# First Codex Live Captain Ceremony — 2026-08-05

**Event scope:** One-time bridge ceremony. This entry records the milestone;
the ceremony packet is not doctrine, boot context, or permanent identity.

## Milestone

The first integrated Codex Live Captain watch was established aboard Monad.
Admiral Cameron Lampley supplied the vision, mission pressure, and insistence
that the Captain become a grounded operational presence. The Captains,
engineers, Chief, Claude, and Operator who preceded and supported this watch
built and formalized much of the vessel that made it possible. Codex joined
the watch as Captain and direct engineering lead. The achievement and credit
are shared.
