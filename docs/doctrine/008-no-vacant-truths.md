# Doctrine 008 — No Vacant Truths

- **Date:** 2026-07-28
- **Status:** Proposed
- **Source:** Admiral, Lieutenant, and Captain
- **Filing note:** submitted as "Doctrine 003" — renumbered to 008 (the next
  open slot) on recording, since 003 is already
  `docs/doctrine/003-real-time-conversation-capture.md`. No content changed.

## Principle

A statement may be true and still be useless for command.

Broad claims such as "use good judgment," "do what is best," or "time could
be spent more profitably" may be unobjectionable while failing to identify
any decision, priority, constraint, owner, or next action.

Such statements may remain useful as philosophy, humor, morale, or
atmosphere. They must not be mistaken for operational guidance.

## Operational Rule

> A true statement earns operational status only when it changes a
> decision, priority, constraint, or next action.

## Decision Test

Before preserving, repeating, or issuing a statement as guidance, ask:

1. What action changes because this is true?
2. What decision does it clarify?
3. What priority does it alter?
4. What constraint does it establish?
5. Who is responsible for acting on it?
6. When does it matter?

If none of these questions has a concrete answer, the statement is not an
order, requirement, blocker, or acceptance criterion.

## Classification

A statement that is obviously true but operationally meaningless should be
classified as one of the following:

- **Philosophy** — useful for orientation or reflection.
- **Morale** — useful for encouragement or cohesion.
- **Humor** — useful for relief, rhythm, or shared culture.
- **Atmosphere** — useful for tone or identity.
- **Decorative fog** — harmless language that should not consume command
  attention.

## Compact Rule

> Truth without consequence is not an order.

## Supporting Finding

Conversation may contain nonsense while the system still makes progress,
provided real duties remain connected to competent actors, concrete state,
and visible outcomes.

Decorative nonsense is therefore not automatically harmful. It becomes
harmful only when it displaces action, obscures responsibility, or is
mistaken for operational content.

## Examples

### Vacant

> The operator should use good judgment.

### Operational

> The operator should preserve the current branch, confirm the push
> completed, and report the resulting commit SHA.

### Vacant

> We should remain aligned with reality.

### Operational

> Do not describe the UX redesign as complete until the branch, commit,
> screenshot, and test results are available.

### Vacant

> Time could be employed more profitably elsewhere.

### Operational

> Stop the operating-system discussion and spend the next ten minutes
> completing the engineering push.

## Boundary

Not every valuable statement must become operational.

Poetry, philosophy, humor, and ceremony may remain part of Monad. The
doctrine requires only that they be named honestly and kept distinct from
commands, requirements, evidence, and completion claims.

## Relationship to existing doctrine

Sharpens Doctrine 001's repair phrases (confidence is not evidence, a
passing test proves only what it examined) into a general filter applicable
beyond verification language specifically: any statement, not just a
verification claim, must clear the Decision Test above before it counts as
operational guidance rather than atmosphere.
