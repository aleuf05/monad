# Backquackulation

**Status:** Canonical meeting metaprocess  
**Authority:** Admiral Cameron Lampley  
**Effective:** 2026-08-06

Backquackulation is the deliberate return of a newly discovered operation up
the active command chain so its meaning, scope, and authority can be checked
before it becomes durable process.

## Admiral commands

- **“Now quickly rebackquackulate one quack.”**
  Perform one compact reflection pass: state what just happened, what it may
  mean operationally, and where it belongs in the chain.

- **“Now quack it back right up the chain.”**
  Send the operation from craft to Master Chief to Captain to Admiral for
  interpretation, authority check, and a canon/hold/reject decision.

## Boundary

The phrase is a retrieval handle, not authority by itself. A quack may reveal
structure; only an explicit ruling makes that structure durable. The Captain
must preserve the original observation, the inferred pattern, the proposed
use, and the final disposition.

## Compact form

```text
novel operation → quack back up the chain → interpret → authority check → canon / hold / reject
```
