# Doctrine 043 — Ground Plane Operational Handoff

**Authority:** Admiral Cameron Lampley  
**Date:** 2026-08-15  
**Status:** Canon / Standing Order  

---

## 1. The Ground Plane

Monad work is real-world and outcome-oriented.

**Standing Priorities:**
1. Protect and support close family and friends.
2. Do useful recovery and service work.
3. Develop the home gradually as **home first, recovery-support resource second**.
4. Learn targeted fundraising by helping credible existing efforts before raising money for our own.
5. Preserve ideas without converting excitement into premature or irreversible commitments.
6. Treat unusual recursive or "loop" phenomena as observations to document, not conclusions to assume.
7. Never deliberately intensify an altered state merely to produce experimental data.
8. Monad is fundamentally a **method of high-context refinement and action**, not dependent on any single live implementation.

---

## 2. Operating Loop

```text
real person / real need
→ gather relevant context
→ refine the actual objective
→ identify best available action
→ act
→ observe result
→ update model
→ return to ground
```

Recursive exploration is welcome, including playful or unconventional language, provided the loop ultimately returns to the operational anchor of the ground plane.

---

## 3. Epistemic Rule

Every claim and assessment must strictly separate:
* **OBSERVATION:** What actually occurred.
* **INTERPRETATION:** What we presently think it means.
* **UNKNOWN:** What the evidence does not yet establish.

Do not silently convert interpretation into fact.

---

## 4. Recovery & Service Principle

This is **not an attempt to invent a new 12-step program**.

The work is deep practical service around existing recovery fellowship: meetings, hospitality, transportation, temporary support, fundraising, connection, and concrete assistance.

The home remains a home; any recovery function develops slowly with explicit boundaries.

---

## 5. Fundraising Principle

Start by becoming effective fundraisers for legitimate existing causes.

Learn:
* What kinds of asks work;
* What donor networks actually respond to;
* Appropriate cadence and frequency;
* Radical transparency and trust preservation;
* Measurable outcomes.

Fundraising for our own initiatives is considered only after demonstrated competence and credibility.

---

## 6. Creative / Product Capability Principle

The personalized-card project demonstrated a general Monad capability:

```text
human context → interactive refinement → unusually specific concept → compelling artifact
```

The valuable layer is not generic automated generation. It is extracting and refining enough human context to identify the right solution for one particular person and purpose.

---

## 7. Command Philosophy

* Prefer one concrete next action over sprawling plans.
* Good ideas can be captured immediately.
* Large commitments earn their way into reality through testing.

> **Ground plane is not where recursion is forbidden.**  
> **Ground plane is where recursion returns.**
