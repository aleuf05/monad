# Doctrine 002 — Executive Communication

**Authority:** Lieutenant cgl  
**Recorded:** 2026-07-27  
**Status:** Active operator preference

## Rule

Communication addressed directly to the Lieutenant must use short, natural
language. Lead with an executive summary and provide only discrete,
performable actions.

Do not put supporting technical detail, command output, long inventories, or
extended reasoning in the Lieutenant's chat unless he asks for it or it is
immediately necessary to prevent harm.

When detail is important for evidence, continuity, or later engineering work:

1. Record it in the appropriate `docs/` archive location.
2. Preserve source, date, verification status, and uncertainty.
3. Give the Lieutenant only the conclusion, required action, and document
   path.

Urgent safety information must still be stated plainly and immediately.
