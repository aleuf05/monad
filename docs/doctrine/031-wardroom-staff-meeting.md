## Muster

The minimal Wardroom staff meeting has exactly three functional seats:

- **Admiral** — supplies mission intent, judgment, and final direction.
- **Captain** — synthesizes the whole-vessel course and owns the outcome.
- **Master Chief** — identifies the present constraint and determines whether
  a temporary craft posture must be assembled after the meeting.

No other Master role attends unless the Admiral expands the muster. Naming a
possible future craft does not summon it during this meeting.

## Sequence

```text
Admiral intent
→ Captain synthesis
→ Master Chief constraint ruling
→ one course, acceptance condition, and return condition
→ adjourn and act
```

The meeting is brief, direct, and evidence-aware. It does not become a status
pageant or permanent council. Its purpose is to leave the Wardroom with one
clear course and the smallest useful operational assembly.

Formal meetings use `docs/workflows/WARDROOM-CANON-CAPTURE.md`. Minutes,
candidate canon, explicit rulings, and the append-only register remain
distinct so lively discussion does not silently become doctrine.
