## Draft interpretation

**Historical claim:** Cameron has accumulated enough operational practice to
maintain continuity and command while inventing the UX live with Claude and
the Captain.

**Inference:** This may be the first realization of the Admiral's promise:
Monad is not merely a store of completed work or a sequence of isolated agent
turns. It becomes a live interaction in which human judgment, continuity, and
AI capabilities specialize through the interaction itself.

**Proposed term:** *interactional specialization* — roles become more precise
through live collaboration around a shared artifact, rather than being fully
specified in advance.

**Metaphor / coined expression:** *monadilicalishously* — the felt quality of
Monad becoming itself through lively, mutually responsive specification.
