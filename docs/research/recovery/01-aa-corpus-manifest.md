## 5. Architectural Rules & Catalog-Driven Expansion

1. **Semantic Authority:** All Big Book citations reference the standard Fourth Edition pagination (Roman numerals for front matter, pp. 1–164 for core text, pp. 165–575 for personal stories).
2. **Copyright Compliance:** In accordance with Title 17 U.S.C. and A.A.W.S. fair use guidelines, Monad maintains conceptual mappings, analytical summaries, structural outlines, and indexed citations. Full-text dumps of copyrighted Fourth Edition materials are strictly prohibited.
3. **Catalog-Driven Expansion:** Future additions to the recovery corpus are selected systematically from the official A.A. literature catalog (General Service Conference-approved pamphlets, guidelines, service manuals) rather than random web scraping.
4. **Epistemic Discipline:**
   - **OBSERVATION:** Verbatim literature text, Conference resolutions, historical publication dates, structural guidelines.
   - **INTERPRETATION:** Semantic analysis of Step mechanics, organizational dynamics, cognitive/behavioral correlations.
   - **UNKNOWN / HYPOTHESIS:** Proposed integration with modern neuroscience, psychological paradigms, or operational models.
