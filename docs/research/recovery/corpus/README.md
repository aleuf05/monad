## Provenance & Textual Lineage

1. **Original 1939 First Edition:**
   - 400 pages, original "circus" / red cover.
   - Contains the original text of Chapters 1–11 ("Bill's Story" through "A Vision for You") and 29 personal stories of early members.
   - Text established the original phrasing of the Twelve Steps, including the foundational Oxford Group lineage and the vital qualifier "God *as we understood Him*".
2. **Textual Variants (1st Ed 1939 vs. 4th Ed 2001):**
   - The core text of the first 164 pages remains structurally identical in argument, chapter sequence, and Step formulation.
   - Pagination in the Fourth Edition standardizes the Doctor's Opinion as Roman numerals (pp. xxiii–xxxii) and core text as pp. 1–164.
3. **Usage Rules:**
   - Always cite official Fourth Edition pagination (pp. 1–164) when communicating semantic references.
   - Use the 1939 First Edition text for programmatic corpus ingestion and structural parsing.
