# 1939 First Edition Structural Breakdown & Public Domain Text Map

**Source:** *Alcoholics Anonymous: The Story of How More Than One Hundred Men Have Recovered from Alcoholism* (Works Publishing Co., 1939)  
**Status:** Public Domain  
**Verification Hash Standard:** SHA-256 Text Block Verification  

---

## 1. Front Matter & Introductory Sections (1939)
* **Foreword to First Edition (1939):**
  - Stated purpose: To show other alcoholics precisely how the first one hundred men recovered.
  - Anonymity statement: Declares that early members remain anonymous at the public level to prevent commercialization and personality inflation.
* **The Doctor's Opinion (Dr. William D. Silkworth, M.D.):**
  - Establishes the medical thesis: The phenomenon of physical craving (allergic reaction) + mental obsession.
  - "The message which can interest and hold these alcoholic men must have depth and weight."

## 2. Core Chapters (1939 Text Lineage)

### Chapter 1: Bill's Story
* Oxford Group connection via Ebby Thacher.
* Towns Hospital detoxification under Dr. Silkworth (December 1934).
* The formulation of ego-deflation and spiritual reliance.

### Chapter 2: There Is A Solution
* The fellowship as an ecosystem of common peril and common solution.
* Carl Jung’s consultation with Rowland H. regarding vital spiritual experiences.

### Chapter 3: More About Alcoholism
* The case of Jim (whiskey in milk), Fred the accountant, and the jaywalker analogy.
* Eradication of the reservation that one may one day drink safely.

### Chapter 4: We Agnostics
* Decoupling spiritual faith from dogma: "God as we understood Him."
* The human spirit as an untapped internal reservoir.

### Chapter 5: How It Works
* The 12 Steps enumerated.
* The actor and the stage metaphor for self-will.
* Three-column moral inventory schema (Resentments, Fears, Sexual Conduct).

### Chapter 6: Into Action
* Step 5 (confession), Steps 6 & 7 (defect removal), Steps 8 & 9 (amends).
* Step 10 daily spot-checks.
* Step 11 morning prayer/meditation, midday pause when agitated, evening review.

### Chapter 7: Working With Others
* 12th Step methodology: Approaching prospects, establishing peer rapport, avoiding moralizing.
* Complete focus on the welfare of the new person.

### Chapters 8, 9, 10, 11:
* To Wives (relational restitution).
* The Family Afterward (spiritual living at home).
* To Employers (economic transparency).
* A Vision for You (establishing local fellowship).
