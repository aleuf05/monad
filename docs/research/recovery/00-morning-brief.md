### 1. What Materially Changed Our Understanding?
* **Medication & A.A. Authority (P-11 / P-87):** The Conference-approved literature is unequivocal: taking prescribed psychiatric or somatic medications under an informed physician does *not* compromise A.A. sobriety. A.A. members are explicitly barred from offering medical advice.
* **Addiction vs. Alcoholism Scope (P-35 / Tradition 3 & 5):** A.A. protects its singleness of purpose to survive. Dual-addicted members are 100% welcomed if they desire to stop drinking, but A.A. is not a general addiction clinic.
* **The Nature of 12-Step Mechanics vs. Engineering Loops:** In engineering, failure triggers a smarter, more optimized strategy; in 12-Step recovery, persistent failure reveals that *self-management and the delusion of control must be surrendered*. Non-action, pausing, and humility are functional operational tools, not passivity.
* **Institutional Boundaries (H&I / BTG):** A.A. 12th Step panels and Bridging the Gap are strictly non-professional, voluntary, non-residential, and non-financial. They connect patients to meetings, period.
