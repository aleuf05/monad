# Project Monad — Human State Safety Policy

**STATUS: DRAFT — FOR POSTERITY AND LATER REVIEW**

This document records the current safety intent. It is not medical advice, not
a legal instrument, and not authorization for the CLI to make autonomous
decisions.

**Source:** Direct human wording from Lieutenant cgl  
**Recorded:** 2026-07-27  
**Status classification:** Draft; not canon  

## Purpose

Prevent any altered mental state, including mania, psychosis, severe sleep
loss, intoxication, medication disruption, or extreme agitation, from becoming
a pathway to unsafe project action.

## Core rule

No single mental state may authorize consequential action.

## Operational safeguards

### 1. Read-only by default

During any period of unusual intensity, reduced sleep, racing thoughts,
grandiosity, paranoia, confusion, or impaired judgment:

- no deployments
- no credential changes
- no spending
- no external announcements
- no hardware control
- no destructive commands
- no changes to production, networking, permissions, or canon

### 2. Two-person approval

Any consequential action requires:

- the human operator to be stable and rested
- a second trusted person to review the action
- a written description of expected effect and rollback

### 3. Delay rule

High-impact actions must wait at least one sleep cycle after they are proposed.

### 4. Stop conditions

Immediately stand down if any of the following appear:

- less than normal sleep
- racing or looping thoughts
- feeling uniquely powerful or invulnerable
- belief that ordinary limits no longer apply
- difficulty distinguishing evidence from interpretation
- urges to escalate systems, spending, or publicity
- medication changes not directed by a clinician

### 5. Safe mode

In safe mode, permitted work is limited to:

- reading
- note-taking
- organizing documents
- drafting noncanonical material
- discussing plans
- ordinary self-care

### 6. Human contact

Keep a current list of:

- treating clinician
- trusted family member or friend
- emergency contact
- local urgent-care or crisis resource

### 7. Evidence standard

Classify claims as:

- observed
- documented
- inferred
- uncertain
- unverified

### 8. No experiments

Do not intentionally induce altered states through:

- sleep deprivation
- substance use
- medication manipulation
- repeated cognitive looping
- prolonged overstimulation

### 9. Emergency rule

If the operator feels unable to stop, is losing touch with reality, or may
become unsafe:

- stop all project activity
- get another person physically present
- contact the treating clinician or urgent medical care

## CLI behavior

When warning signs appear, the CLI should:

- refuse consequential actions
- recommend safe mode
- ask for confirmation of sleep, medication adherence, and human support
- preserve work without executing it
- wait for explicit human reauthorization after stability returns

## Standing doctrine

Protect the human first.  
Preserve the system second.  
Resume slowly.
