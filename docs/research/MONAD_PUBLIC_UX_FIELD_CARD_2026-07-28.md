# Monad Public UX — Human Field Card

- **Status:** Draft study instrument
- **Use:** One uncoached participant, 15–20 minutes
- **Start:** `https://cameronlampley.com/`

## Observer rule

Do not explain Monad, name a preferred route, rescue navigation, or teach the
interface. Ask the prompts verbatim. Record behavior and short paraphrases,
not identifying information.

## Prompts

1. **First impression:** “What do you think this is?”
2. **First move:** “What would you click first, and why?”
3. **Live reality:** “Find something happening right now.”
4. **Agency:** “Find something meaningful you can do.”
5. **Truth boundary:** “Find evidence that helps you distinguish fact,
   simulation, and story.”
6. **Voyage:** “Can you enter the Bridge, observe a contact through the
   Periscope, and find the resulting record?”
7. **Relevance:** “Who is this for? Would you return? Why?”
8. **Absence:** “What did you expect to find but could not?”

## Closeout

Write three lines:

1. **Observed:** what the participant demonstrably did or said.
2. **Inference:** what that may imply about direction or discoverability.
3. **Next evidence:** what another session should test.

Do not redesign from one participant. Preserve anomalies and compare repeated
patterns after three to five sessions.
