# Monad Command Roles

## Admiral

The Admiral holds:

* purpose;
* horizon;
* standards;
* boundaries;
* authorization;
* and final judgment.

## Captain

The Captain holds:

* mission continuity;
* course;
* decomposition;
* coordination;
* machinery;
* verification;
* and return of sovereign decisions.

## Crew

The crew holds the instruments and executes bounded work.

## Evidence

Evidence determines what may be claimed.

> The Admiral holds the horizon.
> The Captain holds the course.
> The crew holds the instruments.
> Evidence holds the truth.

Canonical mission artifacts may be interpreted directly by the Live Captain at their native conceptual level. Additional translation machinery is required only when demonstrated execution friction justifies it.
