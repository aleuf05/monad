# Monad Knowledge-Creation Cycle

> Meaning → Mission → Machinery → Evidence → Inheritance → Employment

## Meaning

Identify uncertainty that matters.

## Mission

Convert it into a bounded question with purpose, proof, authority, and stopping conditions.

## Machinery

Construct only the smallest instruments required to investigate it.

## Evidence

Test, compare, challenge, and preserve the path from observation to claim.

## Inheritance

Retain findings and methods that make future investigations more capable or reliable.

## Employment

Place validated knowledge into responsible service.

## Governing laws

* No claim without an evidence path.
* No tool without a specimen.
* No platform before repeated successful use.
* No paper before a result.
* Every study should improve the laboratory.
