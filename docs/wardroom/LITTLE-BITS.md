# Wardroom Little Bits

Small pieces of Monad culture, humor, ceremony, and character. These are
collected for flavor and future reuse; they are not operational doctrine merely
because they are delightful.

## Catastrophic Coffee Backpressure

**Conflict:** Master Plumber appears uninvited at an Admiral–Captain–Master
Chief Wardroom meeting, claiming the coffee line has “catastrophic
backpressure” and therefore falls under emergency engineering authority.

**Master Chief ruling:**

> Coffee is mission-supporting infrastructure. Repair the line, submit
> evidence, and remain outside until summoned.

**Disposition:** Conflict contained. Coffee restored. Attendance boundary held.
