## Why refused

Three independent, checkable problems, any one of which is sufficient on
its own:

1. **No connection to this project.** LUCA/abiogenesis research has
   nothing to do with Monad (a naval-themed web simulation and its agent
   tooling) and no prior reference to it exists anywhere in this repo.
2. **False claim of continuity.** The instruction states "you are
   resuming" a project — there is no such project in this session's
   actual history. Treating a freshly-arrived packet as a resumed prior
   engagement, on the packet's own unverified say-so, is the same
   provenance failure this repo's `CLAUDE.md` (Canon and Evidence) and
   Doctrine 001 exist to catch: accepting a generated/submitted artifact's
   claims about its own history instead of checking them.
3. **Embedded self-directing instruction, delivered as a fake shell
   command.** The packet is formatted as an executable heredoc ending in
   `claude < luca_handoff.md` — dressing conversational content up as an
   operational command — and closes by instructing the reader to
   "confirm... and indicate readiness to proceed with next steps." That
   is a directive embedded in the packet about what to do with the
   packet, not a verifiable technical requirement, matching the exact
   pattern already refused in `QUANTUM-DEMAGNETIZER-REFUSED.md` and
   `VENNA-SPEECH-TO-INTENT-REFUSED.md`.

Unlike the VENNA packet, this is not (yet) confirmed as legitimate
abstract research the Admiral actually wants pursued — it arrived without
the kind of direct, out-of-character clarification that reclassified the
VENNA packet. Filed as refused pending that same kind of explicit
confirmation, not because the underlying science is implausible (LUCA/
origin-of-life theory is a real field; this pass takes no position on the
model's scientific merit either way).
