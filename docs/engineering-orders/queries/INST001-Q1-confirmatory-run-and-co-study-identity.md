## Q1 — Which real-data triple? *(blocking the confirmatory run)*

The next run must use real data; author-generated curves cannot test the
hypothesis. Choosing the triple is exactly the step where the §1 selection
defect bites, so I do not think the person who wrote the harness should also
pick the domains unilaterally.

**What I need:** either (a) a nominated triple from the Nexus Captain, or
(b) authorization for me to select from public datasets against a stated
criterion, with the criterion fixed in advance.

**My recommended criterion if (b):** the three domains must share **no
measurement apparatus, no citation overlap, and no common units**. That is an
auditable test of "materially different," which the packet leaves undefined.

## Q2 — Who is the "live Captain" in the co-study? *(protocol-level)*

The packet instructs "the live Captain" to derive independently and then
compare against the static Captain via ΔR. In this repository "Live Captain"
names **two different things**: the CLI agent posture (me, and the parallel
Codex and Gemini embodiments), and the running service at
`tools/live-captain/` on port 4778.

I proceeded as the CLI embodiment because the packet arrived in this session.
If the intent was for the **service** to derive, then the derivation was
performed in the wrong body and ΔR would be computed across the wrong pair.

**What I need:** a ruling on which embodiment is the co-study's live arm.
Cheap to answer, expensive to get wrong twice.

## Q3 — Ratification of a methods change I already made

I withdrew the preregistered Π₀ (unbounded power law) as inadequate and
recorded a replacement requirement: **baselines must match inductive bias, not
merely parameter count.** Recorded as amendment 1, append-only; nothing
overwritten.

This is reported for ratification, not permission — it was reversible, it is
documented, and leaving a known-broken baseline in place to await a reply
would have corrupted every subsequent number. But it does change the
instrument's definition, so it should not stand on my say-so alone.

**Recommended promotion:** make the surrogate-beats-baseline check a
**standing precondition** — if the structure-destroyed arm beats Π₀, the
baseline is broken and Q is not reported at all. Costs nothing, and it is what
caught this.

demonstration of how such a claim fails.

**What I need:** whether the Nexus Captain wants the framing repositioned to a
methods contribution now, or held until a real-data triple has run. This
changes what gets built next, not merely what it is called.
