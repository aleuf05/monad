# Live Audio Acceptance

Use this checklist at the end of a Live Captain audio commissioning pass.

## Levels

### 1. Occurrence

The Admiral hears a sound produced by the system.

Record: `audio occurrence: PASS` or `UNCONFIRMED`.

### 2. Intelligibility

The Admiral can understand the Captain’s spoken content without reconstructing
it from context.

Record: `intelligibility: PASS` or `UNCONFIRMED`.

### 3. Conversation usefulness

The Admiral can exchange at least one reliable back-and-forth turn with the
Captain, including turn completion and a comprehensible response.

Record: `conversation usefulness: PASS` or `UNCONFIRMED`.

## Rule

Never promote a lower level into a higher one. A sound can occur without being
understandable; understandable speech can still fail as a usable conversation.
