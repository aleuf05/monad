# Wardroom Canon Capture

**Status:** Active workflow  
**Governing doctrine:** 012 read-before-filing · 013 append-only history · 031 Wardroom Staff Meeting

## Purpose

Convert a formal meeting into trustworthy artifacts without treating every
remark, source packet, or attractive formulation as canon.

```text
meeting record → candidate ruling → Admiral judgment → conflict check
→ doctrine or hold → append-only register
```

## Distinct artifacts

1. **Meeting record** preserves what occurred, including decisions, actions,
   corrections, gates, and the Captain's readback.
2. **Canon candidates** preserve proposed durable rules and their state:
   `CANDIDATE`, `HOLD`, `CANON`, `REJECTED`, or `SUPERSEDED`.
3. **Canon Register** indexes how an explicit ruling reached doctrine. It is
   provenance, not doctrine itself.

Temporary preparation packets remain outside the record unless introduced,
quoted, or ruled upon during the meeting.

## Curious phrasing protocol

When the Admiral uses playful, unusual, or semantically charged phrasing,
capture it first as an observation. Preserve the exact wording, surrounding
exchange, and any recurrence or operational effect. Do not immediately infer
hidden meaning, intention, or doctrine. An interpretation becomes a separate
candidate only when evidence accumulates or the Admiral explicitly asks for
analysis.

```text
curious phrase → exact observation → context / recurrence → possible clue
→ explicit interpretation only when warranted
```

## Live classification

| Kind | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Observation | Checkable statement; attach evidence or mark `UNVERIFIED`. |
| Interpretation | Explanation of observations. |
| Proposal | Possible future course or rule. |
| Ruling | Explicit Admiral judgment and scope. |
| Action | Assigned work with an acceptance condition. |
| Gate | Uncertainty that conditions later action or ruling. |

## Promotion test

Promote a candidate only when authority is explicit, scope is clear, the rule
is durable, factual premises are verified or separate, conflicts are resolved,
the doctrine destination is known, and later revision remains append-visible.
Otherwise preserve it as `HOLD` or meeting material.

## Captain filing sequence

1. Capture the meeting in real time without interrupting natural discussion.
2. Frame the smallest candidate language requiring Admiral judgment.
3. Record the ruling; never infer approval from enthusiasm or silence.
4. Reconcile evidence and check existing doctrine for conflict.
5. File `CANON` language in the smallest appropriate doctrine seam.
6. Append its provenance to the Canon Register.
7. Link actions to implementation evidence and acceptance results.
8. Read back rulings, flexible practice, actions, and gates before adjournment.

## Quality rule

> Minutes preserve what happened. Canon preserves what governs. The register
> preserves how one became the other.
