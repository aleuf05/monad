# Extracted Protocols — Voice Mode Confusion Recovery

**Status:** Draft / proposed (Doctrine 003) — not canon. Requires an
explicit Admiral ruling before either protocol is treated as governing
anything in this repo.

**Provenance:** Extracted from four recovered fragments of a separate,
external ChatGPT session ("Voice Mode Confusion" — see
`logs/captains/2026/2026-07-30_voice-mode-confusion-session-recovery.md`
and its transcript companions). The source session itself is not
authoritative — most of it was ungrounded, escalating chat momentum
("truth singularity," "prove me wrong," etc., see the credibility triage
in that recovery log). These two elements are the exceptions: internally
coherent, self-contained, and independently defensible regardless of
where they came from.

**Why record this:** per Doctrine 003 capture thresholds — "terminology
whose meaning could otherwise drift" and "verified technical findings."
Neither of these is verified yet in the sense of having been tried
against real Monad work; they're recorded as well-formed candidates, not
as adopted practice.

## Packet 1 — Evidentiary Verification Chain

**Epistemic label:** Conjecture (candidate project doctrine, unadopted)

**Input:** A claim of unknown reliability.
**Transform:** Pass the claim through six ordered links:

```
observation → physical record → human interpretation
  → physical action → measurable environmental change
  → independent verification
```

**Boundary condition (invariant):** every link must have a plausible
*physical* causal mechanism. A link may not rest on symbolism, stated
intention alone, hidden agency, or self-reference. A chain that needs
any of those to hold together is invalid at that link, not merely weak.

**Output:** A claim that either survives the full chain (provisionally
trustworthy) or fails at an identifiable link (rejected at that specific
point, not vaguely "doubted").

**Why it's credible on its own terms:** it's the same session's own
antidote to the grandiosity happening around it elsewhere in that
transcript — a real, generalizable skepticism checklist that doesn't
depend on trusting the source it arrived from. Comparable in spirit to
this repo's existing "AI drafts, humans approve canon" boundary in
Doctrine 003; both exist to stop momentum from being mistaken for proof.

**Candidate use in Monad:** could function as a checklist for
"technical finding" claims before they're epistemic-labeled as such in
captain logs — i.e., before writing "Technical finding," walk the claim
through this chain and note where it stops.

## Packet 2 — Caution, Not Hesitation

**Epistemic label:** Conjecture (candidate operating heuristic,
unadopted)

**Definition, as extracted:**

- **Caution** is disciplined forward motion: act in small reversible
  steps, observe the effect, then continue or stop based on what was
  observed.
- **Hesitation** is stalled motion: delay driven by treating uncertainty
  itself as intolerable, rather than by any specific unresolved risk.

**Boundary condition:** neither extreme is caution —
"wait for perfect certainty" and "rush because the idea feels urgent"
are both excluded as failure modes, not just hesitation's failure mode.

**Why it's credible on its own terms:** it matches this repo's own
existing bold-execution policy almost exactly (`CLAUDE.md`: "Prefer
rapid, reversible implementation over exhaustive validation... don't
spend more time proving a change works than implementing it") — not
copied from it, arrived at independently in a completely different
conversation, which is a small point in its favor rather than evidence
it's derivative.

**Candidate use in Monad:** as stated language for the existing
bold-execution posture, if the Admiral wants a compact phrase for it —
"caution, not hesitation" captures the same intent as the existing
policy prose in six words.
