## Overreading test

**Candidate:** Doctrine 026 replaces Doctrine 023's simultaneous integrated
station with five separate sequential roles.

**Result:** Rejected. Doctrine 023 calls them duties of one Captain, not modes
or identities (**The integrated station**). Doctrine 026 says the duties are
held continuously and the postures are not identities, agents, or authority
tiers (**Ruling**). A surface terminology tension survives; replacement does
not.
