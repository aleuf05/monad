# Historical Continuity

Monad preserves its design history because future engineers need to understand
not only what the system is, but why it took its present form.

## Evolution of the center of gravity

1. **Persistent engineering workspace.** Early Monad material emphasized a
   durable place for AI-assisted software work and runnable experiments.
2. **Maritime command environment.** The Bridge, instruments, fleet state, and
   FleetCore supplied a coherent interaction model and a visible shared world.
3. **Command and institutional memory.** Charters, doctrine, reports, mission
   records, and living-memory work clarified authority, provenance, and
   continuity across sessions.
4. **Cognitive landscape.** The project increasingly centered on preventing
   valuable thought and engineering work from becoming fragmented or buried,
   with private command and public experimentation treated as distinct layers.
5. **Semantic Artifact Engineering.** Current research treats artifacts as
   projections of refined semantic representations, allowing images,
   diagrams, notebooks, interfaces, and documents to develop from shared
   intent and knowledge.

These stages are cumulative rather than mutually exclusive. The workspace,
command interfaces, memory systems, and maritime world remain active
mechanisms; the current mission explains what they serve.

## Reading earlier formulations

- Preserve original wording, date, provenance, and status.
- Do not silently rewrite a historical packet to sound current.
- Mark a formulation as superseded only when a later authorized record clearly
  replaces it; otherwise describe it as an earlier, parallel, provisional, or
  draft framing.
- Link forward to the current mission while retaining backward provenance.
- Treat implementation reports as evidence of their date, not timeless claims.

## Current interpretation

The [Project Mission](../mission.md) is the current documentation-level center
of gravity. It does not erase the older aspirational mission language or
component-centered descriptions. It places them in context: aspiration records
the horizon, while the current mission explains Monad's practical organizing
purpose and the mechanisms by which it is pursued.
