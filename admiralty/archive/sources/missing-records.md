# Missing Records and Candidate Recovery

The following phrases or principles were requested for investigation but were
not promoted to canon merely because they were named in the build order:

- Minimal Activation Doctrine;
- Narrative follows reality;
- No Ghost Systems;
- AI drafts, humans approve canon;
- Health outranks mission;
- least-privilege operating principles;
- immutable or inspectable logging requirements;
- shutdown, stop, and escalation rules;
- Bedroom Containment Boundary.

Some may exist under different names in the indexed corpus. Until a direct
source and status are located, each is **candidate recovery** or **uncertain**,
not an adopted doctrine.
