# Current Mission

**Status:** Current documentation framing; source: [`docs/mission.md`](../../../docs/mission.md)

> Monad organizes Cameron's cognitive landscape so valuable engineering work
> is preserved, connected, refined, and recoverable instead of becoming
> fragmented or buried.

Supporting mechanisms include the persistent workspace, memory/archive,
command interfaces, research methods, and public/private projections.

**Boundary:** Preserving a thought does not approve its execution, publication,
canon promotion, or deployment.
