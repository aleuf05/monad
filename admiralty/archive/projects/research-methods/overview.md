# Research Methods

## Executive Summary

Monad's research-methods program develops ways to preserve intent, integrate
knowledge, refine semantics, generate projections, and review evidence.

## Why It Matters

Methods are becoming the project's center of gravity: improving representations
can improve many artifacts without editing each artifact independently.

## Current Status

Active research corpus with mixed draft, proposed, and reference status.

## What Exists

Semantic Artifact Engineering, IntentForge, Operational Topology, latent-space
research, truth/session methods, and archive proposals.

## What Is Demonstrated

Concept packets and selected experiments exist; general effectiveness is not
yet established.

## What Is Not Yet Demonstrated

That any one formalism is a validated universal architecture.

## Major Decisions

Preserve uncertainty; review semantic fidelity; keep human approval boundaries.

## Risks and Limitations

Semantic interpretation can become unfalsifiable without reuse, prediction, or
explicit review criteria.

## Open Questions

How should semantic and operational improvements be measured?

## Recommended Next Action

Use small, evidence-producing slices with clear provenance and acceptance tests.
