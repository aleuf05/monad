# Bridge

## Executive Summary

Bridge Station is the flagship command-deck projection composing browser
instruments and engineering/status context.

## Why It Matters

It demonstrates how multiple instruments can be presented as one navigable
operational surface.

## Current Status

Implemented public/browser artifact; exact active variant is date-sensitive.

## What Exists

Bridge Station documentation, deployed toy assets, and site navigation records.

## What Is Demonstrated

Composition of fleet, periscope, watch, and status views is documented.

## What Is Not Yet Demonstrated

That Bridge is the private Admiralty Archive or Root Console.

## Major Decisions

Component consolidation records identify the current Bridge Station path.

## Risks and Limitations

Public presentation must remain distinct from private memory and authority.

## Open Questions

Which Bridge elements, if any, should project into Admiralty?

## Recommended Next Action

Keep Bridge as an instrument/projection while the Admiralty surface remains
strategic and private.
