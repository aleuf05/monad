# Beastscape

## Executive Summary

Captain reconstruction describes a visual and navigational environment with
generated creatures and an adaptive user-specific navigator.

## Why It Matters

It may test structural exploration, semantic projection, and lived navigation.

## Current Status

Research/prototype direction; evidence quality mixed.

## What Exists

Beastscape research, UMAP/latent-space tooling, and dated foundry records.

## What Is Demonstrated

Research slices and visual experiments are documented; a complete product claim
is not established.

## What Is Not Yet Demonstrated

That an adaptive navigator reliably improves human understanding or relevance.

## Major Decisions

Atlas-first and structural latent-space ideas remain research directions.

## Risks and Limitations

Interpretive richness can outrun evidence and causal legibility.

## Open Questions

What minimum experiment demonstrates useful navigation rather than spectacle?

## Recommended Next Action

Review the smallest evidence-producing slice before adding complexity.
