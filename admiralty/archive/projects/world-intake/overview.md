# World Intake

## Executive Summary

Review-oriented intake path for proposed world or mission material, preserving
source evidence and routing decisions to the owning system.

## Why It Matters

It tests the distinction between a proposal, review, accepted record, and
public projection.

## Current Status

Implemented/design-complete components are documented; live status is mixed.

## What Exists

Architecture, engineering orders, verification findings, tools, and public
review projection records.

## What Is Demonstrated

Review cards, provenance, and source-owned decision paths are documented.

## What Is Not Yet Demonstrated

That all intake paths share one complete canonical review workflow.

## Major Decisions

Public review aggregation must not become a new decision endpoint.

## Risks and Limitations

Concurrent sessions and stale projections have caused documented incidents.

## Open Questions

Which intake records should be visible in private Admiralty by default?

## Recommended Next Action

Link pending review counts into Admiralty without duplicating source authority.
