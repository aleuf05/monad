# Periscope

## Executive Summary

Canvas-based optical watch instrument rendering a maritime view and vessel
contacts from shared state when available.

## Why It Matters

It is a distinct sensory projection of shared world state.

## Current Status

Implemented browser instrument with multiple dated versions.

## What Exists

Periscope source, requirements, engineering reports, and deployed projection.

## What Is Demonstrated

Rendering and state-derived contact display are documented.

## What Is Not Yet Demonstrated

That visual contact display provides strategic command understanding by itself.

## Major Decisions

Periscope remains an instrument, not the command authority layer.

## Risks and Limitations

Shared-state drift and asset/deployment divergence are documented risks.

## Open Questions

How should lived experience and causal explanations accompany visual state?

## Recommended Next Action

Use as a projection reference for causal-legibility work.
