CAPTAIN AUTONOMOUS RESEARCH PACKET 01
Continuous Strange-Loop Machine
Program: Project Monad
Inquiry class: Bounded exploratory research arc
Authority: Captain may continue unaided within the stated envelope
Research object: A continuously evolving strange-loop organism whose discrete outputs are observations sampled from an underlying flow
1. Founding principle
Continuity Primacy of Looping
Looping is treated as fundamentally continuous. Discrete ticks, events, reports, and telemetry are useful implementation and observation mechanisms, but they must not be mistaken for the ontology of the loop itself.
The candidate machine is therefore modeled as an evolving dynamical system:
[ \frac{d\mathbf{x}}{dt}
F(\mathbf{x},\mathbf{s},\mathbf{m},\mathbf{e}) ]
where:
\mathbf{x}: lower-level organism state;
\mathbf{s}: self-model state;
\mathbf{m}: memory and historical traces;
\mathbf{e}: environment;
F: mutually reentrant dynamics.
Discrete observations are sampled:
[ \mathbf{y}_n=H(\mathbf{x}(n\Delta t)) ]
The sample is evidence about the loop, not the loop itself.
2. Primary research question
What is the minimum continuous dynamical architecture capable of sustaining a causally active, historically developing, necessarily incomplete model of itself?
3. Competing hypotheses
H1 — Continuous closure
A genuine strange-loop regime can emerge from continuous reciprocal coupling among behavior, self-model, memory, and environment.
H2 — Sampled discrete sufficiency
The apparently continuous behavior is fully explained by sufficiently fine discrete updates. Continuity adds no scientifically meaningful organization.
H3 — Hybrid necessity
Fast continuous dynamics support ordinary cognition, while slower discrete events are required for durable self-model revision and autobiographical binding.
H4 — Decorative flow
Continuous visual or numerical activity may look organism-like while failing to generate downward causation, historical self-revision, or productive self-opacity.
4. Minimal candidate architecture
The first continuous machine should contain four coupled fields.
Activity field X(t)
Represents lower-level behavior, resource regulation, perception, and action tendencies.
Self-model field S(t)
Represents the organism’s compressed prediction of its own activity and causal organization.
Memory field M(t)
Accumulates, strengthens, weakens, and compresses traces over time.
Salience and concern field V(t)
Determines which errors and internal changes become globally consequential.
Candidate coupling:
[ \begin{aligned} \dot X &= f(X,E,S,V)\ \dot S &= g(S,X,M,O)\ \dot M &= h(M,X,S,V)\ \dot V &= j(V,E,X,S) \end{aligned} ]
where O represents estimated self-opacity.
The important feature is reciprocal dependence. No field should merely display another field’s state without affecting its future development.

6. First buildable experiment
Construct three matched systems.
C0 — Continuous reactive system
Continuous lower-level activity with no self-model.
C1 — Continuous decorative mirror
Continuous activity and a self-model field, but no downward influence from S to X.
C2 — Continuous causal looper
The self-model influences lower-level dynamics, and resulting changes revise the self-model and memory.
Apply a temporary hidden alteration to one internal coupling coefficient.
Measure whether each system:
detects prediction divergence;
reallocates modeling activity;
produces an informative intervention;
revises its self-model;
retains the revision;
responds differently to a related later disturbance.
7. Candidate observables
The Captain should derive and refine measures for:
self-model prediction error;
downward causal efficacy;
field coherence;
attractor residence;
recovery time;
historical dependence;
self-model allocation;
opacity calibration;
transfer after prior self-misdiagnosis;
sensitivity to sampling rate;
divergence between internal flow and sampled report.
A critical question is whether important dynamics disappear when the system is observed too coarsely.
8. Required comparison with Monad-0
The continuous machine must not merely replace the discrete apparatus by proclamation.
Compare both systems on:
causal interpretability;
deterministic replay;
computational cost;
transition sharpness;
historical persistence;
perturbation recovery;
representational richness;
artificial-life scalability;
susceptibility to measurement artifacts.
The discrete Monad-0 apparatus remains the control laboratory.
The continuous machine is the candidate deeper organism.

10. Natural stopping conditions
Return to the Admiral when any one occurs:
A minimal architecture is sufficiently precise for implementation.
Two equally credible architectures require a command-level choice.
The continuous hypothesis collapses into ordinary discrete computation with no meaningful distinction.
A novel ethical issue appears.
A mathematical obstacle makes implementation substantially different from the original vision.
Evidence suggests the discrete and continuous programs should merge.
Further progress requires live experimental data.
The research begins repeating itself without increasing discrimination.
