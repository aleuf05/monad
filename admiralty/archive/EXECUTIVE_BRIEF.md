# Executive Brief

**Status:** Derived first brief; awaiting Admiral review where marked

## What is Monad now?

Monad organizes Cameron's cognitive landscape so valuable engineering work is
preserved, connected, refined, and recoverable instead of becoming fragmented
or buried. Its workspace, memory, command surfaces, research programs, and
public instruments are mechanisms supporting that mission.

## What major systems exist?

The repository contains FleetCore's deterministic maritime world model, browser
instruments including Bridge Station and Periscope, Living Fleet and World
Intake services, archive/context tooling, and research programs including
Semantic Artifact Engineering and Operational Topology. The source inventory
and project briefs link the evidence for each.

## What is presently canonical?

The dated Monad Charter, adopted/active doctrine explicitly marked by its
source, and directly recorded implementation evidence remain the strongest
authorities. The current mission and archive summaries are current framing;
they are not a replacement for explicit human canon promotion.

## What is actively being built?

The Admiralty Archive itself is being seeded as an executive layer. The
Admiralty UI brief defines a static or lightly functional strategic surface.
Project-level activity varies; see [current project state](executive/current-project-state.md).

## What is uncertain or possibly abandoned?

Several older component directions and mission descriptions coexist in dated
records. The archive does not infer abandonment from silence. Conflicts and
uncertain status are listed in the [Decision Queue](DECISION_QUEUE.md).
