Executive Summary

Abstract

Contemporary autonomous agent architectures suffer from systemic epistemic degradation: unanchored conversational drift, hallucinated authority boundaries, and the loss of human intent across session transitions. In this paper, we introduce Monad-1, an operational topology and algebraic verification framework designed to transform transient human sparks into durable, verifiable operational warp. Monad-1 formalizes knowledge evolution as an invariant-preserving state transition Mt → Mt+1 strictly bounded by four deterministic continuity predicates: Identity (I), Epistemic Termination (T), Hypergraph Reachability (R), and Cryptographic Governance (G).

The Capture Effect: Formal proof and empirical demonstration that the naming, digest-sealing, and topological integration of human intent irreversibly alters system state space without requiring lossy retraining.
Zero-Drift Continuity: Deterministic evaluation over an active 352-document repository maintaining sub-millisecond rollback guarantees and strict reversibility bounds.
