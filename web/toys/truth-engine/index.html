The Truth Engine

A disciplined path from observation to understanding. Not an oracle that declares truth, and not a skeptic that says no to everything — a navigational instrument that keeps curiosity alive, certainty honest, and action grounded. Describe something that happened; the engine separates fact from interpretation, generates competing explanations, designs tests that could defeat each one, and recommends only the action the evidence actually justifies.

Observe → Separate fact/interpretation → Competing hypotheses → Disconfirming tests → Confidence, visibly → Act at the level evidence justifies → Preserve anomalies
