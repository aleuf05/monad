# Assumptions and boundary

This is an explanatory upper-bound model, not a propulsion-system design.

- Motion is one-dimensional and non-relativistic.
- Gravity, drag, staging, structural changes, and external forces are absent.
- All propellant mass becomes reaction mass.
- The available energy is \(R_e(m_i-m_f)\).
- Energy can be stored and reassigned between reaction-mass parcels without
  storage mass, loss, power limit, or thermal constraint.
- Exhaust velocity is measured relative to the instantaneous vehicle for the
  differential rocket equation.

The broader source notebook explores stored-energy requirements and the
equivalence of the optimal schedule to coherent final exhaust motion. Those
topics are intentionally outside the narrow Version 1 interaction.
